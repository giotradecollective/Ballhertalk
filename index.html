Meet the hosts

Four friends, one group chat, way too many opinions about the offside rule.

Khanyisile Zungu

Meet Khanyisile Zungu, just a girl who fell in love with football and believed that women deserved a space where they could talk about the game without feeling like they had to prove they belong.

Ball Her Talk is more than a dream, it’s a vision she could no longer keep to herself. She chose to step out in faith because she knew it was bigger than her, bigger than the four of them. She is nervous, excited, and, above all, grateful for the opportunity to bring it to life.

Welcome to Ball Her Talk.

Lutendo “Luu” Ravele

Some people light up a room the moment they walk in. Lutendo is one of those people.

She brings calm, honesty, and a perspective that reminds us football isn’t just about the 90 minutes, it’s about the stories, the people, and the emotions that come with it.

She asks the questions we’re all thinking, speaks with intention, and isn’t afraid to see the game differently.

Meet Lutendo “Luu” Ravele, our co-host, football lover, and an important voice behind Ball Her Talk.

Onthatile “Nthats” Lesotho

Introducing Onthatile “Nthats” Lesotho, an avid sports lover, passionate football enthusiast, and firm believer that the beautiful game is even better when it's shared with the girls.

With a personality full of good vibes, laughter, and authenticity, Nthats brings a refreshing energy that turns football conversations into a safe space where women can celebrate the game, share their opinions, and embrace the beauty of girlhood.

This space is where football meets girl talk, where every opinion matters, every laugh is welcome, and every football-loving girl feels like she belongs.

Gugulethu “Lethu” Ndhlovu

Introducing Gugulethu “Lethu” Ndhlovu.

A football enthusiast with a love for meaningful conversations and the beautiful game in all its forms.

Whether it's matchday debates, transfer window drama, or simply celebrating the passion, culture and moments that make football so special, Gugulethu believes football has the power to connect people and create a community. Football is about the stories, the emotions, and the people who make every match unforgettable.

Through Ball Her Talk, she's excited to bring honest conversations, fresh perspectives, and a space where women can enjoy the game unapologetically. This is more than just football—it's passion, community, and girlhood, all in one place.

“We started BallHerTalk because we were tired of being the only women in the room who actually watched the match.”

BallHerTalk began as a group chat that got too big for group chat energy. What started as post match voice notes turned into a full podcast: part tactics breakdown, part girl talk, all football.

Every week we cover the matches, the transfer window chaos, and the culture around the game, plus the conversations that never make it onto the panel shows. No mansplaining the offside rule required.
